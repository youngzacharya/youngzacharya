PhD Candidate in History at the University of Florida
======
I am a specialist in medieval and early modern Jewish Christianity. While my dissertation project focuses on the ritual and practice of early 15th century Jewish Christians (viewed through the prism of the theological writings and pastoral practice of the rabbi-turned-bishop Pablo de Santa Maria), my research interests include the history of Catholicism and medieval/early modern religious history more broadly. My teaching portfolio includes both survey courses and more narrowly focused courses on the history of sacred violence and of evangelization.
